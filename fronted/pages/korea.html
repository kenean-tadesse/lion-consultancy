INTERNATIONAL ADMISSIONS

Daejin University

Admission Requirements • Spring 2027

International Students Freshman & transfer pathways
Language Requirements Korean or approved English qualifications
  • Applicant must be a foreign national whose parents are both foreign nationals.
  • Applicant must have completed, or expect to complete, the required regular elementary, middle and high-school curriculum in Korea or abroad.
  • Dual or multiple nationalities involving the applicant or either parent are not recognized as foreign-national status for this admission category.
  • Applicants who acquired foreign nationality after birth are not recognized as foreign nationals for this category.
  • Freshman applicants must meet the university's academic eligibility requirements.
  • Applicant age must be 25 or younger.

Applicants may satisfy Korean-language eligibility through one of the approved pathways below.

TOPIK TOPIK Level 3 or higher. Level 2 or higher may apply to Arts and Physical Education majors.
King Sejong Institute Intermediate 1 or higher.
KIIP KIIP Level 4 or higher.
University Korean Program Completion of an approved Korean-language program through the university's Korean Language Education Center, including applicable 6-month, 2-semester or 400-hour pathways.
Korean Assessment Passing the Korean-language assessment administered by the University's Korean Language Education Center.
Certain qualifying applicants may be admitted conditionally and required to complete a special Korean-language or designated Korean Studies program during the first year.
IELTS 5.5+
CEFR B2
NEW TEPS 202+

Applicants from countries where English is a native or legally recognized official language may qualify for an exemption by providing acceptable proof of completed secondary or higher education. For countries where English is an official language, the curriculum must have been taught in English.

International students must satisfy at least one approved Korean-language graduation requirement.

TOPIK Level 4 before graduation. Level 3 may apply to Arts and Physical Education.
King Sejong Institute Intermediate 2 or higher.
KIIP KIIP pre-assessment 81+ or KIIP Level 4 or higher.
Approved bilingual programs, including English-track and Chinese-track programs, are exempt from the Korean-language graduation requirement.

Documents not issued in Korean or English generally require an appropriately notarized Korean or English translation. Original-document requirements apply.
  • Applications are submitted through the official Study in Korea online system.
  • Applicants must submit required admission documents and pay the application fee by the applicable deadline.
  • Original documents are required where the admissions guidance specifies originals.
  • False information, forged documents or fraudulent applications may result in cancellation of admission or enrollment.
  • Applicants admitted to multiple universities may register at only one university.
  • Visa issuance is determined by the relevant authorities. Admission does not guarantee visa issuance.
  • Additional supporting documents may be requested during document review.
  • Transfer students must satisfy the university's required remaining semesters according to their admission year.
Important The official Korean admissions guidelines prevail if there is any discrepancy between an English translation and the official Korean version.
