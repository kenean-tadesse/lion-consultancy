PLAN YOUR BUDGET

Think beyond tuition fees.

Studying abroad involves more than the university application. A realistic financial plan should consider the full student experience.

Tuition

Tuition depends on the university, program, degree level and other institutional factors. Always confirm the current amount directly with the institution.

Accommodation

Plan for accommodation costs, utilities and other housing-related expenses before making your final study decision.

Living Expenses

Consider food, transportation, communication, study materials and everyday personal expenses.

* Costs and immigration requirements can change. Students should verify current information with the relevant university and official Hungarian authorities before making financial or travel decisions.
